Traffic sources and crash rates

AADT means annual average daily vehicle traffic. It is not a live count. The collection year and estimation method appear in each section’s details.

2024 adds modeled local-road volumes absent from 2023. They are excluded by default. Source categories use the provider’s published field definitions.

Traffic-adjusted crash rates are not yet calculated: inventory versions do not establish annual vehicle exposure for the crash dates. This is also not a measure of pedestrian or bicycle exposure.

About this analysis +

2024–2026 records are provisional. Recent reporting is incomplete. Recent-data snapshot: November 4, 2026. Historical 2014–2015 records added November 5, 2026.

Annualized counts divide crash totals by time. They do not measure traffic exposure or crash risk.

Whole-road totals match normalized reported roadway names. Intersection records may match either named road. Numbered routes also use distance to their centerlines. Segments require coordinates within the chosen distance; unlocated crashes cannot be assigned to a segment.

Road geometry: MassDOT 2025 inventory. Areas: MassGIS 2022 ward/precinct boundaries.
